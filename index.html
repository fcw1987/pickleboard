Help & Information

How to Use

  • Drag player icons and ball around the court
  • Double-tap player icons to switch orientation (left/right)
  • Cross the net - Players change teams automatically
  • Draw - Toggle drawing mode with ✏️ button
  • Singles/Doubles - Switch game modes in menu
  • Reset - Return tokens to default positions
  • Theme - Toggle light/dark mode

Court Layout

  • Grey area: Non-volley zone (Kitchen)
  • Navy blue area: Service boxes
  • Net runs through the center
  • Court is 44' x 20' regulation size
  • Players/ball can move outside court boundaries
