Help & Information

How to Use

  • Drag branded green/orange players and the ball around the court
  • Teams - Green faces Orange from opposite ends of the court
  • Handedness - Players start right-handed; double click on desktop or double tap on touch devices
  • Draw - Toggle drawing mode with the ✏️ button
  • Singles/Doubles - Modes may change team color while preserving handedness
  • Reset - Return players and ball to default positions without changing handedness
  • Theme - Toggle light/dark mode

Plays

Plays demonstrate common patterns rather than mandatory strategy. Choose one from the menu, then use Previous, Play/Pause, Next, Restart, or Exit to study it.

While a Play is open, choose 3D View for a spatial replay. Switch cameras, use Play/Pause or Restart, choose a playback speed, and Exit 3D to return to the same 2D Play.

Court Layout

  • Grey area: Non-volley zone (Kitchen)
  • Navy blue area: Service boxes
  • Net runs through the center
  • Court is 44' x 20' regulation size
  • Players/ball can move outside court boundaries
